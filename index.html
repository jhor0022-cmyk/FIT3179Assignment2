06 / TAKEAWAY

Conlcusion

Total emissions, per-person emissions, changes over time and cumulative emissions answer different questions. Together they show why climate action needs both the largest emitters and the highest-emitting populations to chnge, while recognising historical differences and national circumstances.

DATA & METHODOLOGY

Sources, definitions and limitations

Charts are built from locally bundled CSV snapshots of published datasets, with no invented or imputed values. Country and year coverage varies by indicator; missing records are omitted. Figures from different publishers may use different methodologies

  • Annual CO₂ emissions — Our World in Data / Global Carbon Budget. Million tonnes of fossil-fuel and industry CO₂, excluding land-use change.
  • CO₂ emissions per capita — Our World in Data / Global Carbon Budget. Tonnes of CO₂ per person per year.
  • Cumulative CO₂ emissions — Our World in Data / Global Carbon Budget. Historical running totals, subject to revisions.
  • Sector emissions — European Commission JRC EDGAR / IEA-EDGAR CO₂. Original figures were converted from Gg to Mt and grouped into eight sectors; international aviation and shipping are not allocated to countries.
  • World map geometry — world-atlas / Natural Earth. Geographic boundaries are simplified for visualisation.

Dataset preparation: Data files and human-readable Vega-Lite chart specifications are bundled with this website. Data source pages should be checked for later revisions. The page loads visualization libraries and map geometry from public CDNs.
